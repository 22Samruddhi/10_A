About KBTCOE

Karmaveer Adv. Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering, Nashik is an autonomous engineering college under MVP Samaj. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs with modern laboratories and experienced faculty.

Vision

To become a globally recognized institute producing competent engineers.

Mission

  • Provide quality education.
  • Promote research and innovation.
  • Develop ethical professionals.
